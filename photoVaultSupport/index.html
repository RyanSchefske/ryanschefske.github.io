Frequently asked questions

I forgot my passcode. Can you reset it?

No — and this is deliberate. Your photos are encrypted with a key that only your passcode (or Face ID / Touch ID on your device) can unlock. We never receive your passcode and have no way to recover it. Nobody can, including us.

If you set up Cloud Backup and saved your recovery code, tap Forgot Passcode? on the lock screen. You'll enter the recovery code and choose a new passcode, and your backup replaces what's on the device.

If you have no recovery code, the vault cannot be opened. This is the tradeoff that makes the app trustworthy.

What is the recovery code, and why does it matter so much?

When you turn on Cloud Backup, Vaulted shows you a one-time recovery code. It's the only key to your encrypted backup. We never store it and cannot regenerate or look it up.

Write it down and keep it somewhere safe and separate from your phone. If you lose it, you can generate a new one from Settings → Cloud Backup → Generate New Recovery Code while you still have access to your vault.

How do I restore my photos on a new iPhone?

  1. Install Vaulted on the new device and sign in to the same iCloud account.
  2. On the first screen, tap Restore from Backup.
  3. Enter your recovery code and choose a new passcode for this device.

Your photos, albums, and videos download and decrypt on the device.

My backup says it's on, but nothing is uploading

Backups are manual — they run when you tap Back Up Now in Settings → Cloud Backup. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and have space available. If you see an error, the message will say what went wrong.

Can I use Vaulted on my iPhone and iPad at the same time?

Not yet. Vaulted currently supports one device, with backup and restore for moving to a new phone rather than live syncing between devices. Backing up from two devices to the same iCloud account isn't supported and can overwrite the other device's backup.

Why won't it import my video?

Files larger than 512 MB aren't supported in this version. Support for larger videos is planned.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Everything you've already stored stays in your vault and remains accessible. Pro-only settings (calculator lock screen, face-down lock, disguised icon) revert to their defaults.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Refund requests go through Apple, not through us.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code in the app. Your photos never reach any server we operate. Optional iCloud backups go to your private iCloud database as encrypted data we cannot read. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Please email ryanschefske@gmail.com. Bug reports that include your iOS version and steps to reproduce are especially helpful.
